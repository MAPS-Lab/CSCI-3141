## Sharing and attribution

Unless the instructor explicitly marks material private or restricted, released
course materials may be shared with credit to the author and CSCI 3141. Preserve
source links, third-party attributions, and applicable licence terms. Copyright
remains with the respective owners.

Students must keep assessed work private before its submission deadline. Public
portfolio sharing and in-class presentation begin after the deadline when the
instructor opens sharing, following the syllabus. Grades, feedback, personal
information, and instructor-marked private materials remain private.
